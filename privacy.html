Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026. This page covers the buku.pro website. The buku.pro apps keep their own privacy terms inside each product.

Who we are

buku.pro operates this site and is the data controller for the personal data described on this page. Our data protection contact is support@buku.pro.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site. When you join a waitlist we receive your email address, alongside the consent tick that sends it. When you ask about DentalOS AI through the lead form we receive your work email address. If you email us, we receive what you send.

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and runs no third-party trackers.

Why we collect it

To do the one thing you asked: tell you when a product opens, or reply to your enquiry. We do not add you to anything else, and we do not sell, rent, or share your details with anyone for their own marketing.

Consent and unsubscribe

Waitlist forms on this site ask for your consent before anything is sent, under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010. Every message we send tells you how to stop receiving them. You can also unsubscribe at any time by emailing support@buku.pro.

Where your data lives

Form submissions are processed on our behalf by form-processing service providers, and this static site is served by website hosting providers. We keep your email only while your request is active or your consent stands. If a breach ever affects your details, we will notify you and the regulator as Malaysian law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, ask us to move a copy of it to another provider, or withdraw consent, by emailing support@buku.pro. We will respond and act on every request.

Changes to this policy

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so here and update the date at the top.
